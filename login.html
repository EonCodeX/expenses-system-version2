📖 How to Use Expense Monitor

Quick guide for members — register, sign in, and track expenses

1
Create an Account Click Create Account below. Choose your household group, pick a username & password, then submit.
2
Wait for Admin Approval Your account starts as Pending. The admin will approve it from the Users page. You'll be able to sign in once approved.
3
Sign In Enter your username and password here. Default admin credentials: Username / Password
4
Track Bills & Loans Go to Bills to add monthly expenses and mark them paid. Go to Loans to track borrowed money and log payments.
💡 Tip: Change the default admin password after your first login via the sidebar → Change Password
