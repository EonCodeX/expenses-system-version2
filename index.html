📖 How to Use Expense Monitor

Step-by-step guide from login to managing transactions

🔐

Step 1 — Sign In

Open the app at http://localhost:3000 and sign in with your username and password. First-time default: admin / admin123

💡 Tips

  • Default admin password is admin123 — change it after first login
  • If your account shows "Pending", wait for the admin to approve it
  • Forgot password? Ask the admin to reset it from the Users page
📝

Step 2 — Create an Account

New members click "Create Account" on the login page. Fill in username, choose your household group, set a password, and submit. Your account waits for admin approval.

💡 Tips

  • Username: 3–20 characters, letters/numbers/underscore only
  • Password must be at least 6 characters
  • Select the correct household group — this determines which bills you see
📊

Step 3 — Dashboard Overview

After login you see the Dashboard — summary cards showing total bills, unpaid amount, overdue count, and charts. Use the cut-off filter to view 1st or 2nd half of the month.

Step 4 — Managing Bills

Go to Bills from the sidebar. Add a bill with the ＋ Add Bill button. Mark bills as Paid using the ✓ button. Upload proof of payment with 📎.

💡 Tips

  • Click ✓ on a row to quickly mark a bill as Paid
  • Click 📎 to upload a photo of the receipt as proof
  • Set Recurrence (monthly, weekly) and bills auto-copy each period
  • Switch to 📅 Calendar view to see bills on a calendar
💳

Step 5 — Loan Tracker

Go to Loans to track borrowed money. Add a loan with lender, principal, and monthly payment. Click 💳 Pay to record each payment and watch the progress bar fill up.
